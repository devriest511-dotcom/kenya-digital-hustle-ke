PRIVACY

Privacy notice

This starter website stores saved opportunity IDs and submitted opportunity suggestions in your current browser using local storage. In this demo, that information is not sent to a server, and submissions are not visible to the platform owner from another device.

External links

Opportunity links open third-party websites. Those sites have their own privacy policies and terms. Check the official source before sharing personal information.

Before launch

A production version should replace browser-only storage with a secured database, publish a full privacy policy explaining data collection and retention, and provide a contact address for privacy requests.

Contact: Add an official project contact before public launch.
